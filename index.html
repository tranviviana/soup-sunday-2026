There will be red spatter. The lab has not yet determined whether the substance is blood.

The recipe

Ingredients — scaled for 6

  • 4½ lb mixed fresh tomatoes, halved if bigger than 1"
  • 6 medium shallots, peeled and halved
  • 4½ Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2–3 heads of garlic
  • 1½ tsp kosher salt, ⅜ tsp black pepper
  • 1½ cups packed fresh basil
  • ¾ cup chicken stock
  • ¾ cup heavy cream, plus more to serve

Method

  1. Oven to 425°F. Halve any tomato bigger than an inch; peel and halve the shallots.
  2. Cut the tops off the garlic heads to expose the cloves, drizzle with oil, wrap each in foil.
  3. Spread everything across two sheet pans and toss with the oil, salt and pepper. Tuck the garlic in.
  4. Roast 35–40 minutes, until the tomatoes collapse and the shallots go soft.
  5. Squeeze the garlic out of its skins into the pot, add everything from the pans plus the basil and stock.
  6. Blend smooth. Stir in the cream. Season.

Adapted from Well Seasoned Studio, scaled to 1.5×. Made the day before, which makes it better.

Clay Day

Sunday 4 October 7 seats

The night

Everyone gets the same character, pulled out of a hat at the start, and sculpts their own version of it. Seven wildly different attempts at one thing, lined up at the end.

The menu

Sandwich
Roast beef, provolone & dijonOn ciabatta, pressed. Horseradish on the table if you want it.

Eat first, sculpt second.

The recipe

Ingredients — scaled for 8

  • 28 oz mixed fresh mushrooms
  • ½ cup dried porcini
  • 8 cups chicken stock
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • ⅔ cup heavy cream
  • 2–4 Tbsp olive oil, parsley, salt and pepper

Method

  1. Cover the porcini with hot water and leave them 20 minutes.
  2. Soften the onions in oil over medium heat, 5–7 minutes.
  3. Add the fresh mushrooms and cook about 10 minutes, until they've given up their water and shrunk right down.
  4. Garlic and thyme, one minute more.
  5. Add the porcini and their soaking liquid — pour carefully and leave the grit behind.
  6. Stock in, simmer 10–15 minutes. Blend smooth, stir in the cream, season.

Adapted from Inside the Rustic Kitchen, scaled to 2×.

Pumpkin Carving

Sunday 25 October 7 seats

The night

Carving, on every flat surface I own, with newspaper under all of it. The messiest night of the season by a wide margin.

Bring your own pumpkin and that covers your $8. Pick whatever shape speaks to you. There will be a lot of guts.

The menu

Sandwich
Apple & smoked goudaOn sourdough. Salt and smoke against a sweet soup.
The recipe

Ingredients — scaled for 8

  • 1 very large or 2 medium butternut squash, peeled, seeded, 1" cubes
  • 3 Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled, 1" pieces
  • 4½ Tbsp olive oil, divided · 1½ Tbsp butter
  • 2 yellow onions · 2 celery ribs · 2 large carrots
  • 1½ Tbsp fresh thyme
  • 6 cups chicken broth, or more
  • Heavy cream to drizzle, salt and pepper

Method

  1. Oven to 400°F. Toss the squash and potatoes with 3 Tbsp of the oil across two sheet pans, season well.
  2. Roast 30–35 minutes, stirring halfway, until tender.
  3. Meanwhile melt the butter with the rest of the oil over medium. Cook the onion, celery and carrot 7–10 minutes until soft. Thyme in, season.
  4. Add the roasted squash and potatoes, pour in the broth, simmer 10 minutes.
  5. Blend until creamy, adding broth if it's too thick — squash and potato make this one heavy.
  6. Thyme on top, cream drizzled over.

Adapted from Delish, scaled to 1.5×. Made entirely ahead — the night's work is the carving.

Parent Trap

Sunday 1 November 7 seats

The night

The movie, and friendship bracelets while it plays. Talking over it is encouraged — that's the whole reason your hands are busy.

The menu

Soup
Chicken vegetableBrowned thighs, shredded back in, with carrot, celery, zucchini, cumin and a lot of lemon.
Sandwich
Twin grilled cheesesCheddar on one, smoked gouda on the other, cut in half and swapped so nothing matches.
Dessert
Oreos and peanut butterNon-negotiable.

Everything comes in twos except one thing. That's the movie, the sandwich, and the bracelet you're about to give away.

The recipe

Ingredients — scaled for 8

  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1½ cups each diced carrot, celery and onion
  • 6 cloves garlic, minced
  • ¾ cup zucchini · ¾ cup bell pepper, diced
  • 6 bone-in chicken thighs
  • 9 cups chicken stock · 1½ tsp cumin
  • 1–2 lemons, juiced · parsley and cilantro

Method

  1. Season the thighs. Heat the oil over medium-high and brown them on all sides. Set aside, keep the drippings.
  2. Medium heat. Onion, carrot and celery until softened.
  3. Bell pepper, zucchini, garlic and cumin, about 2 minutes.
  4. Heat up, stock in, bring to a simmer. Chicken back in, drop to low.
  5. 10–15 minutes until cooked through. Lift the thighs, shred the meat off the bones, return it.
  6. Off the heat, stir in the herbs and cover. Lemon juice to taste.

Adapted from Kitchen on Fire, scaled to 1.5×. No sausage.
